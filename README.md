# ML Threat Remediation — Case Study (Cofense)

> Turning machine learning into a security product customers rely on: 99.998% accurate automated remediation, 66% less alert noise, $116M in pre-launch pipeline and 22% revenue growth in 12 months.

*Public summary of my product leadership work. No proprietary code, data or customer information.*

## Context
Cofense protects enterprises from phishing. Security teams were flooded with user-reported emails, and polymorphic phishing (campaigns that constantly change form) meant many near-duplicate threats needed manual triage. I was Senior Director of Product Management and Head of Intelligence, owning packaging, pricing, roadmap and P&L for a **$130M ARR** SaaS portfolio and leading **17 people across three global departments**: Product Management, Data Engineering and Cyber Threat Intelligence.

## What we built
- **Automated threat triage** (Cofense Triage & Vision): ML and data science that cluster and prioritize reported threats
- **Automated remediation** of polymorphic threats at **99.998% accuracy**
- **Noise reduction**: **66% fewer false-positive alerts** for SOC teams

## How we ran it
| Practice | Why it mattered |
|---|---|
| Product Councils and Customer Advisory Boards | Kept ML priorities tied to what SOC analysts actually needed |
| Data Engineering + Threat Intel in the same org | Labeled threat intelligence fed model quality directly |
| AI-automated roadmap and cross-functional workflows | **+88% delivery speed, –35% operational overhead** |
| Partnership with Customer Success | Turned accuracy gains into adoption and retention |

## Results
| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Remediation accuracy | **99.998%** |
| False-positive alerts | **–66%** |
| Pre-launch pipeline | **$116M** |
| Product revenue | **+22% in 12 months** |
| Enterprise churn | **8% → under 3%** |
| Active platform usage | **+21%** |
| Private-equity ROI | **97%** |

## Lessons
1. **Accuracy is a product feature, not a model metric.** Customers bought fewer false positives, not an algorithm.
2. **Put the data people next to the product people.** Merging Data Engineering and Threat Intelligence under one roadmap sped up every release.
3. **Sell before you ship.** Early customer validation built $116M of pipeline before launch.
